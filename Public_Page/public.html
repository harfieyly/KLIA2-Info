Getting to & from KLIA Terminal 2

KLIA Terminal 2 (klia2) is well-connected to Kuala Lumpur city center and surrounding areas through multiple public transport options. Whether you prefer speed, comfort, or affordability, there's a transport mode to suit your needs. All transport hubs are located at the Gateway@klia2 mall, connected via a covered walkway to the terminal.

KLIA Express

Fastest

Non-stop train service from klia2 to KL Sentral in just 33 minutes.

  • 5:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  • RM55 (one-way)
  • Every 15-20 mins
