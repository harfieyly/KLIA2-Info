Must-Visit Destinations

Petronas Twin Towers

Iconic 88-storey skyscrapers and a world-renowned landmark of KL.

Landmark
Batu Caves

A limestone hill featuring a series of caves and colorful temples.

Culture
Bukit Bintang

The shopping and entertainment heart of Kuala Lumpur city center.

Shopping
Merdeka Square

The historic site where the Malayan flag was first raised in 1957.

History
KL Tower

Offers a stunning 360-degree panoramic view of the city skyline.

Landmark
Central Market

A heritage site and a hub for Malaysian arts, crafts, and souvenirs.

Heritage
Chinatown

Shop for anything from gems and incense to toys and t-shirts, the true allure of this night market is in wandering about and enjoying its sights, sounds and energy.

Shopping
Masjid Negara

Masjid Negara, or National Mosque is an architectural tribute to Islam, the official religion of Malaysia. The Masjid Negara can accommodate up to 15,000 worshippers during prayers.

Culture
Saloma Link

Named after the late Puan Sri Salmah Ismail, better known by her stage name Saloma — a legendary Malaysian singer and actress — the bridge pays homage to her enduring legacy in Malaysian entertainment.
